Our Story

Two Cultures,
One Table

Che Peru was born from a simple belief: the world's two greatest culinary traditions — Peru's vibrant, ocean-driven cuisine and Argentina's centuries-old fire culture — belong on the same plate.

We are a family-owned restaurant, newly opened in the heart of Toronto's Bathurst & Eglinton neighbourhood. Our recipes have been passed down across generations and perfected over thousands of meals — now brought to Canada with the same warmth and generosity that defined Sunday dinners back home.

Every morning, our kitchen fills with the scent of fresh lime, ají amarillo, and slow-burning charcoal. Every evening, our tables fill with neighbours, families, and curious food lovers who leave as friends.

This is not a restaurant. This is a home with a kitchen that happens to be open to everyone.
